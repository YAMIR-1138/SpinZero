How this works

Each tube is a unit vector pointing at its slot. The rotor is balanced when those vectors cancel: Σ e^(i·2π·j/n) = 0. The leftover sum is the imbalance, a vector that points at the heavy side.

When you spin, that heavy side is pushed outward by a force proportional to |Σspin| × RPM². The force sweeps around with the rotor, so the machine traces a small circle, which is the wobble. When the imbalance is zero there is nothing to push, so a balanced rotor stays centered even at full speed.

Auto-Balance uses prime-factor cycle decomposition to place the tubes in a balanced pattern when one exists. Some combinations of n and k cannot be balanced at all; when that happens it says so instead of returning a wrong answer.
